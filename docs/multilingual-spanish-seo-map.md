| vídeos con presentador / portavoz | SERP is AI-avatar tools (HeyGen, Synthesia) and Fiverr listings. | Positioned as real human presenters, explicitly not AI avatars (verified English claim). Low-volume, conversion-supporting page. |
| vídeos explicativos SaaS | Few Spanish agencies; international directories (Advids, Clutch) and a handful of studios (The Video Valley, Venture Videos). | `/es/saas-video-production` targets "vídeos explicativos y demos para SaaS"; a real gap. |
