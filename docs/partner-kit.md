# CONVRT Referral Partner Kit

Sent **by hand, by email, after you have approved a partner**. Nothing here is sent automatically, and the
registration form never emails the partner. The kit is previewed on `/referrals`; the preview text comes from
`src/lib/partnerKit.js` and must match section B and C below (a test fails if they drift apart).

> Before sending: approve the partner yourself, agree the final reward conditions with them (payment method,
> payout timing, attribution) and, for **Agency & Strategic Partners**, confirm the approval explicitly. Do not
> include a Calendly link: partners introduce brands, they don't book calls with you.

## D. What the Creative Upgrade is (and isn't): for partners

**Your Connection Gets More Creative. You Get Rewarded.**
When someone you refer becomes a CONVRT client, you earn a referral reward, and they receive two additional video
ad variations at no extra cost.

- **What they get:** two additional ready-to-test video ad variations on their **first qualifying paid video project**.
- **What the variations are:** edit-based variations made from the project's approved footage, voiceover, graphics and
  other existing assets, such as alternative opening hooks, different first-three-second sequences, revised text
  overlays, different CTA endings or alternative edit structures.
- **What it is not:** a new production. No additional filming, creator sourcing or shoot days, and no fundamentally new
  video concepts are included.
- **Additional to the package:** the two variations are on top of what the purchased package already includes.
  Variations that are already part of the package don't count as the bonus.
- **Not exchangeable for cash**, and it doesn't automatically stack with the Free Video Production Trial. It is separate
  from the Free Creative Growth Roadmap and the 10 Free Ad Hooks.
- **Confirmed before purchase:** CONVRT confirms eligibility and delivery specifications with the brand before it buys.
- **No promises about results.** Never tell a prospect the upgrade will improve their ad performance.

## E. Who to introduce (referral qualification guide)

CONVRT is looking for:

- SaaS and AI SaaS companies.
- E-commerce and DTC brands.
- Businesses already investing in marketing.
- Brands with a genuine need for recurring creative production.
- Teams looking for professional video ads or social content.

Industry alone doesn't make a referral eligible. CONVRT reviews every introduction and confirms eligibility and
attribution. Quality beats quantity: please introduce brands you actually know, not lists.

## F. How to make a referral

Approved methods:

1. **Warm email introduction**: send template B to the brand (disclosure included), then email
   `mohamed@convrt.studio` with the brand's company name and website. Copy the brand's contact only if they have agreed
   to be introduced.
2. **LinkedIn introduction**: send template C, then email CONVRT the company name and website.
3. **Direct introduction to CONVRT**: an email introducing both sides, once the brand has said yes.
4. **Referral submission form, when appropriate**: there is no public referral form yet, so use the email route.

Please don't send CONVRT contact details (email addresses, phone numbers) for people who haven't agreed to be
contacted, and don't do unsolicited bulk outreach on CONVRT's behalf. Self-referrals and misleading submissions aren't
eligible. If the company has already been in touch with CONVRT, tell us. Previously active prospects may be excluded
and CONVRT confirms attribution.

## G. Reward and qualification conditions (summary)

- A reward is earned only when: the introduction is properly attributed to the partner, CONVRT accepts it as eligible,
  the brand becomes a paying client, and its first qualifying payment clears and remains non-refundable.
- Registering, submitting a company name or booking a call doesn't trigger a reward.
- Standard partners: $150 per eligible referral, as cash or production credit. Agency & Strategic Partners: 10% of the
  first qualifying project payment, excluding taxes, refunds and third-party pass-through costs. One-time, not recurring.
  The two can't automatically be combined.
- Payment method and timing are agreed with the partner. There is no self-service dashboard at launch: partners can
  ask CONVRT for a status update.
