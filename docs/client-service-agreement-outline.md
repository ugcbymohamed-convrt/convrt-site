# B2B Client Service Agreement: outline only (NOT published, NOT active, NOT legal text)

CONVRT has **no standard Client Service Agreement yet**. This file lists the clauses such an agreement would need and, for each, what the site already says (so the agreement and the website cannot contradict each other). It is an outline for you and your Spanish legal adviser. Nothing here is drafted contract language, nothing has been approved, and there is **no route, page, link or sitemap entry** for it (a test enforces that). The website Terms of Service stay separate: they cover use of the site and the free offers and say a signed agreement prevails for a paid project.

Legend: **Site** = a term the site already publishes (source in `src/seo/businessKnowledge.js`); **Owner** = a decision only you can make; **Lawyer** = needs drafting by a legal adviser.
